A local guide for everyone

The places that matter, closer to you.

Finding a useful local service should not feel difficult. NearHelp brings nearby places together so you can search by category and explore available results in one place.

Whether you are new to an area, looking for an essential service, or helping someone else, NearHelp is designed to make local information easier to explore.

NearHelp uses available map data. Some places may not appear until they are added to the map data or suggested by the community.

If a useful place is missing, you can submit its details for review. Approved suggestions can help make the directory more useful.

    Use information carefully

    A few things to keep in mind

    NearHelp helps you explore place information. Use your own judgment when making decisions.

    Check important details

    Names, addresses, opening hours, and availability can change. Contact a place directly when you need confirmation.

    Map results may be incomplete

    A place may exist but not appear if it is missing from the geographic map data.

    Location access is optional

    You can decline the browser location request. Nearby search needs coordinates to find places around you.

    Protect your account

    Keep your password private and sign out when using a shared device.

    Frequently asked questions

    Common questions about NearHelp

    Why are no places appearing near me?

    Check that location access is allowed and the correct category is selected. Map data may be incomplete in your area. You can suggest a missing place for review.

    Is every listing guaranteed to be current?

    No. Place information can change, and map data may be incomplete or out of date. Confirm important details directly with the place.

    Do I need an account to search for places?

    Nearby search is available from this page. An account is needed for features such as messaging and suggesting places.

    Why does my browser ask for location permission?

    Nearby search uses your coordinates to look for places around you. You can decline the request in your browser.

    Can I add a place that is missing?

    Yes. Sign in and use the Suggest a place form. Your submission will be reviewed before it is approved.

    Does NearHelp contact emergency services?

    No. NearHelp does not dispatch emergency responders. Contact your local emergency services directly if you need urgent help.

    How do I update my profile?

    Sign in, open your profile, edit the available details, and save your changes.

    How do I contact the NearHelp team?

    Use the email address in the contact section below.
